## FAQ

### How does Quick Shop work?
Choose a store, add products to your cart, enter your delivery address, and send the prepared order through WhatsApp.

### What if I can't find the item I need?
Use Custom Order and type what you're looking for. The request will be included in your WhatsApp order.

### Is there a minimum order?
For normal product-only carts, you need at least ₹200 worth of listed products. If you add a Custom Order, you can continue without meeting the ₹200 minimum.

### Do I pay on the website?
No online payment is required in the MVP. Your order is sent to the store through WhatsApp.

### Has my order been placed when WhatsApp opens?
Not yet. WhatsApp opens with your message prepared. You need to press Send to submit it.

## Tone

The copy should be:
- Friendly.
- Direct.
- Local.
- Trustworthy.
- Short.
- Human.

Avoid:
- Corporate jargon.
- Overly clever slogans.
- Long explanations.
- Fake urgency.
- Excessive emojis.
